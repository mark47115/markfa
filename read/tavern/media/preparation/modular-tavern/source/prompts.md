# 선술집 실내·가구·착석 제작 원본

모델 ID 없이 내장 OpenAI ImageGen을 사용했다. 실내는 [레벨 1 외관](../../level-up/yard-1.png)과 [레벨 2~5 외관 시트](../../level-up/source/yard.png)를 기준으로 제작했다. 가구는 [3레벨 외관](../../level-up/yard-3.png)의 목재·청록 색감을 참고했다. 착석 자세는 실제 프로젝트의 서 있는 도트로 만든 [원본 시트 A](standing-reference-1.png), [원본 시트 B](standing-reference-2.png)에서 순서와 캐릭터를 이어받았다.

가구만 있는 투명 원본에서 각 자산을 추출했다. 착석 결과는 해당 유닛의 원본 팔레트로 맞췄고, 64×64 셀 네 장에서 골반 아래를 고정했다. 레벨별 배경의 2×2 원본은 좌상·우상·좌하·우하 순으로 2~5레벨이다.

## 테이블을 향하는 의자

기존 [정면 의자 원본](chairs.png)을 바탕으로 각 레벨의 옆면과 사선 방향을 만들었다. [방향별 생성 원본](chair-directions.png)의 윗줄은 오른쪽 옆면, 아랫줄은 오른쪽 아래 사선이다. 왼쪽 방향에는 해당 그림을 좌우 반전해 사용한다. 반투명 배경을 걷어내고 기존 의자 팔레트와 48×58 캔버스, 바닥 기준 행 55에 맞춰 내보냈다.

```text
Edit and expand the supplied Cavern Heros wooden chair sprite sheet with two facing directions. Preserve the five level designs exactly: Lv1 plain single-slat oak, Lv2 double slats, Lv3 crossed oak back and teal seat, Lv4 rounded carved back with two teal stripes, Lv5 high arched back with deep teal upholstery and modest golden trim. Create a production atlas with exactly 2 rows and 5 equal columns, one freestanding isolated chair per cell, columns Lv1 through Lv5, landscape 3:2 canvas. In the TOP row physically turn the chairs on the ground 90 degrees to face SCREEN RIGHT: show clearly from the side and slightly above. The tall back posts and backrest are on the LEFT of the seat, the sitting surface projects to the RIGHT, and the near front leg is on the right. In the BOTTOM row physically turn the chairs to face LOWER RIGHT in 3/4 view (45 degrees toward the screen from right). View their front and side together, backrest behind and upper left of seat, front seat edge and front feet lower right. Both rows are upright chairs that would stand on the same floor, not tilted sprites in the image plane. Match the reference palette and hard square nearest-neighbor pixel edges, consistent seat height and size. Each whole chair including feet and back must fit in its cell, generous alpha gutters, no ambient shadow, no painted background, no gradients, text, furniture other than the 10 chairs, people or labels. True transparent alpha.
```

## 착석 유닛 96픽셀 시트

기본 OpenAI ImageGen에서 두 장의 서 있는 유닛 시트와 기존 착석 모음을 참고해 제작했다. 완성용 시트는 4×4 원본에서 유닛을 각각 분리하고, 서 있는 원본의 10색 이하 팔레트와 96×96 셀 네 장에 맞춰 내보냈다. [생성 원본](seated-v2.png) · [생산용 시트 추출 스크립트](pack-seat-sprites.ps1)

```text
Create one production-quality square 4×4 pixel-art atlas for the original game Cavern Heros, with exactly 16 isolated fully seated characters, one per equal cell. True transparent alpha, no grid, no lines, no captions, no labels, no shadows, no furniture. Keep every character at its own cell center horizontally and vertically, full body, with generous margins: sprite occupies roughly central 55% of each cell, so no parts reach or cross a cell border. Use only 8–10 flat color clusters per character, from their existing palette. Actual deliberate hard-edged pixel-art clusters, crisp selected dark silhouette, no gradients or antialiasing, no texture. Slight upper 3/4 view from front-left, ~2 heads tall in a compact sitting pose. Use the first two attached images as exact design, color, facial proportions and signature equipment references, and the third image only to see current sitting poses (ignore the furniture in it). Preserve reference eyes/helmet opening, headgear, dark outlines and held props. Make the head and equipment as clear as the standing references while shortening the body through folded knees, and place all equipment with the character. Avoid adding badges or accessories beyond the references. Seat height and relative framing consistent.

Order left to right. Row 1 is the top row of reference 1: green twig and pale curls seedling keeper, green leaf hood and warm wooden bow sprout archer, cream hood and horizontal tan seedpod trumpet, plum crested knight and broad burgundy/cream crescent shield. Row 2 is the bottom row of reference 1: pale closed helmet burgundy scarf and spear, teal curved cap and angular wooden crossbow, orange-white fox ears and curled tail, round golden automaton with a wrench claw. Row 3 is the top row of reference 2: gold rivet assembly bot with dark horizontal oversized piston tool, grey wolf hood and diagonal javelin, tawny tufted lynx with brown cloak, ivory falcon-headed archer with slate-blue collar and visible folded wing. Row 4 is the bottom row of reference 2: ivory knight with tall plum plume and plum rectangular shield, plum archer cap with off-white angled feather and curved bow, cream-hooded priest with plum collar and cream lamp carried in the hand, squat ivory closed helm with black eye slots, wide plum gorget and golden bell mace. Every one is sitting with hips low and knees folded, and their props either rest at the side or across their lap. The graphic must contain exactly 16 subjects. No separate free-floating elements.
```
